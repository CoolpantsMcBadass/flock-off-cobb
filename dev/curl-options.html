Flyer bottom edge — five treatments

The strip under the sheet is meant to read as the paper curling where it unrolled from. Click a card to unroll it. Judge it in motion — the bar is only convincing while it is moving.

Worth knowing: this was designed when opening was a literal unroll. The shape now morphs from the comic burst straight into the sheet's frame, so the unroll metaphor is weaker than it was — which is why E (none) is on the list. If the answer is "it never reads right", removing it is a real answer.
