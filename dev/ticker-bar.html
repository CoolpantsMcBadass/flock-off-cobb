A · Saying "click me" with no label

All closed, all static, so they can be compared as they would first be met. The tag says whether the signal survives with no pointer, which is the question that matters: this bar exists for the phone. Hover-only signals are not wrong, they are just not enough on their own.

B · How it opens

A nav above and a hero below, because the opening includes what happens to everything else. Push puts the panel in the flow, so the hero is shoved down by the full height of the poster. Overlay drops it over the hero with a scrim and nothing moves. Click a bar to open it, click again or press Escape to close.

Notes worth keeping. The countdown is DST-correct Eastern using the same zoned() helper as the council countdowns in index.html, so it is the real thing rather than a local-time approximation. The bar is a real <button> with aria-expanded and Escape to close, and the event's date, time and place are real text rather than living only inside the poster image, which is what the existing ticker's aria-hidden="true" could not have offered. Every :hover rule here is behind @media(hover:hover) and (min-width:861px), for the reason the README sets out at length.
